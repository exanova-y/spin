e-waste hunting

The best e-waste is found in large quantities in MIT underground tunnels. Contact Ilya Gulko at Harvard street commons, who knows the way.

In UC Berkeley:

  • Wurster Hall — 1st floor. Go under the stairs, past the restrooms and drinking fountains and turn left.
  • Sutardja Dai — 2nd floor. Accessible from the loading area.
  • Davis Hall — 1st/2nd floor
  • Etcheverry Hall — 1st floor. In the hallway, near a bunch of gas cylinders.
  • McCone Hall — G/1st/2nd/3rd floor. Be prepared for a lot of rocks...and some tech too, I suppose.
  • Latimer Hall — B floor. Exit the elevator and turn left. If you turn left again, there's some boxes with free laboratory glassware. If you turn right instead, there's the e-waste bins.
  • BAIR lab — 8th floor. The e-waste quality is not great.
  • Valley Life Sciences Building — 3rd floor, in the cargo elevator room
  • Evans Hall — 4th/9th floor (infrequent)
  • Soda Hall — 1st/2nd/3rd floor (infrequent, but VERY nice when it appears, so still check regularly!)
